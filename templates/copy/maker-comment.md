# Maker comment

Post within 5 minutes of going live. 120–250 words. A person talking, not a press release.

## Anatomy

1. **Why** — the problem as you lived it (1–2 sentences)
2. **What** — what the product does, concretely
3. **Who** — who it's for + one specific first thing to try
4. **Ask** — the exact feedback you want
5. **Offer** (optional) — PH-exclusive deal

## Draft

Hey Product Hunt 👋

<why — the problem as you lived it>

<what — concrete>

<who + the first thing to try>

<the specific question you want answered>

<offer if any>

I'll be here all day — tell me what you think.
