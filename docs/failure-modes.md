# Failure modes

How launches actually die. Each is preventable; most happen because nobody owned them.

## 1. Launching a product that isn't ready

A waitlist, a broken signup, a demo that crashes. PH visitors try the thing *now*. If the product can't be used in the next 5 minutes, delay the launch.

## 2. Missing the first 4 hours

Launching at 9am PST, sleeping through 00:01–04:00, no maker comment for hours. The board's shape is set early — you can't buy the morning back at noon.

## 3. Ghosting comments

Every unanswered comment is a ranking signal lost and a visitor who watched the maker not care. "Thanks!" replies are nearly as bad — substance is the signal.

## 4. Asking for upvotes

The fastest way to get flagged. DMs, group chats, newsletters, "upvote me" tweets — all of it. The allowed sentence is: *"we're live on Product Hunt, would love your feedback."*

## 5. Marketing-speak assets

Tagline is a slogan nobody understands. Gallery is unframed screenshots or stock mockups. Maker comment reads like a press release. If a stranger can't say what the product does after the first gallery image, the page fails.

## 6. Wrong topics / wrong feed

Picking flattering categories instead of accurate ones puts you in digests nobody relevant reads. Reach in the right feed > size of the wrong feed.

## 7. Landing page collapse

Spike traffic hits a slow page, a dead deploy, or a signup wall. The PH bounce is unforgiving — load test, pre-warm, keep the first value free of friction.

## 8. Copy-pasting across channels

PH copy posted verbatim to HN gets flagged as marketing; Reddit buries it. Each channel has a ritual (`channels.md`) — rewrite, don't forward.

## 9. Launching alone in a vacuum

Zero supporters at 00:01 means the first hours are silent, and silence compounds. You don't need a mob — you need 10 people who'll genuinely look and comment. Build that list at T-7.

## 10. Treating launch as the finish line

No badge embed, no postmortem, no repurposing, no T+1 HN. A launch is an asset factory — the traffic spike dies in 3 days, but the badge, backlinks, corpus data, and content live for years. Teams that don't harvest T+1..7 keep less than half the value.
