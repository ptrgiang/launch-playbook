# Channel rules

Every launch surface has its own ritual. Posting PH-style copy to Hacker News or Reddit fails — and can get you banned. Sequence and rewrite per channel.

## Sequencing

```
00:01 PST   Product Hunt goes live + maker comment
06:00–08:00 EU/morning supporters engage; keep replying
07:00–09:00 Cross-post wave: X thread, LinkedIn post
09:00–11:00 Reddit posts (per-sub rules), Indie Hackers
All day     Reply duty on PH — <30 min per comment
T+1         Show HN (separate ritual — don't stack it on PH day)
T+1..3      Badge embed, thank-yous, secondary directories
```

Show HN gets its own day. The audiences overlap less than you think, and each deserves full attention.

## X / Twitter

- Format: a thread — hook tweet, 3–5 tweets of story/demo, closing tweet with the PH link
- Framing: **"we're live" / "check it out" — never "upvote us"** (see `product-hunt.md` anti-gaming rules)
- Pin the thread for the day; quote-tweet your own milestones ("#4 on PH right now")
- Reply to every reply — X rewards the same engagement loop PH does
- Post from the founder account; company accounts underperform

## LinkedIn

- One post, founder profile. Story-led: why you built it, one real moment, the link in **first comment** (LinkedIn suppresses outbound links in-body)
- Lower ceiling, longer tail — B2B products get customers here days later

## Show HN

- Text post. Title format: `Show HN: <name> – <concrete one-line description>`
- Technical angle only: what's interesting about how it's built, what problem it solves. Zero marketing language.
- Be present in comments for hours — HN rewards makers who engage technically and punishes drive-by promotion
- Best window: weekday morning US Eastern. It's luck-weighted; a flop is normal, repost is allowed after ~2 weeks

## Reddit

- Self-promo is allowed **only in specific subs** — read each sub's sidebar rules before posting:
  - `r/SideProject` — always open, project posts welcome
  - `r/InternetIsBeautiful` — sites/tools people can try now
  - `r/alphaandbetausers` — early-stage feedback
  - `r/startups` — self-promo **only on designated days** (check current rules)
  - Niche subs for your product's domain — often the highest-converting
- Title rules: no marketing-speak, state what it is and why it's useful
- Expect blunt feedback; reply as a builder, not a marketer
- Never buy or coordinate votes — Reddit detects and bans permanently

## Indie Hackers

- Milestone + launch post with real numbers. The community rewards transparency — revenue, signups, what you'd do differently
- Works best T+1 as a retro-flavored post

## Directories & secondary surfaces

Post T+1..7, each needs its own submission ritual: BetaList, Uneed, Tiny Launch, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub. Low traffic, real backlinks — this is the SEO tail, not the spike.

## Email / notify list

- Your own list converts better than any channel. Send at 07:00–08:00 PST with the PH link and "feedback wanted" framing
- PH's upcoming/notify features change over time — check what's live before counting on them
