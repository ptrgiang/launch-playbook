# Product Hunt mechanics

How the daily leaderboard actually works, and what that means for your launch.

## The day

- Product Hunt runs on **Pacific Time**. The board resets at **midnight PST**.
- Launch at **00:01–00:15 PST**. A launch at 9am PST has already lost the morning to whoever launched at midnight.
- Best days: **Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday**. Maximum buyer traffic and maximum competition. Weekends are easier to rank #1 but the audience is thinner and the badge carries less weight.
- Avoid: major holidays, Apple/WWDC keynotes, days when a mega-launch (e.g. a well-known company) will eat the board.

## How ranking works

The board is not a pure vote count. Signals that move you up:

1. **Comments and comment velocity** — a launch with 80 real comments beats one with 300 silent upvotes. The first 4 hours set the trajectory.
2. **Upvote quality** — votes from established accounts count more than votes from accounts created today. This is why asking strangers to "make an account and vote" barely works.
3. **Engagement loops** — makers who reply to every comment get more comments. The algorithm rewards the conversation, not just the tally.
4. **External traffic** — visitors arriving from X/LinkedIn/newsletters who engage (comment, try the product) help; drive-by votes help less.

## Featured vs. unfeatured

PH's team decides which launches appear on the homepage ("featured") vs. the "newest" feed. Being featured is the single biggest determinant of your day — unfeatured launches get a fraction of the traffic.

What improves your odds of being featured:

- A complete launch page: gallery filled, maker comment posted within minutes, real demo video, correct topics
- A product that is actually usable today — not a waitlist
- A maker with a real PH account history (comment on other launches in the weeks before yours)
- A product that fits what PH's audience buys: dev tools, productivity, design, AI, consumer apps

## The anti-gaming rules

PH actively suppresses coordinated voting. These get launches flagged, de-ranked, or removed:

- Asking for upvotes — in DMs, group chats, newsletters, or social posts
- Upvote rings, paid upvotes, incentivized voting ("vote and get X")
- Mass signup drives where people register just to vote
- Links shared with "please upvote" framing

What **is** allowed: "we're live on Product Hunt — come check it out." Share the link, ask for *feedback*, let people vote if they want. The phrasing matters; teach it to everyone amplifying you.

## The first 4 hours

00:01–04:00 PST is when the day's shape is set:

- Post your **maker comment immediately** (see `docs/assets.md`)
- Start replying to comments the moment they land — target <30 minutes per comment, substantive replies ("here's why we built it that way"), never "thanks!"
- Wake up your earliest timezone supporters first (Asia/EU are awake while you sleep — queue your posts accordingly or be awake)
- Watch the board hourly; note who's climbing and why

## The rest of the day

- 07:00–09:00 PST: US wakes up. Cross-post wave (see `docs/channels.md`)
- Keep replying all day. Makers who vanish at noon lose ranks they earned at dawn.
- Milestone posts are fine ("we're #3 right now") — never pair them with a vote ask
- 23:59 PST the day freezes. Whatever rank you hold is your headline forever.

## After

- If you finish top 5, you get a badge — embed it on your landing page the same day
- Product of the Day/Week badges are permanent social proof; use them
- Write your postmortem within 48h while numbers are fresh (`docs/postmortem.md`)
- Traffic decays over ~3 days; the SEO and backlink tail lasts much longer
